# AidoLang — Free Bilingual Daily‑Conversation Practice

AidoLang is a browser‑first language‑learning platform focused on real‑world daily conversations.
It provides **325 real‑life dialogue scenes across 15 everyday topics**, supports 12 interface languages, bilingual reading, audio listening, shadow‑reading practice and built‑in quizzes.
No account required, no login wall, no subscription fees. All learning content opens instantly in your browser.

🔗 Official Website: <[https://aidolang.com](https://aidolang.com)>

---

## ✨ Core Philosophy
- **Real‑Scene Oriented**: All dialogues are built around authentic daily scenarios for practical spoken‑language improvement.
- **Bilingual Design**: Side‑by‑side dual‑language text, key‑word highlighting for faster vocabulary building.
- **Audio Listening & Repeat**: Tap lines to play audio; adjustable playback speed for shadow‑reading practice.
- **Zero Registration**: Learn immediately without email sign‑up. Your phrasebook progress stores locally within your browser.
- **Customizable Learning Themes**: 10 visual desktop themes to customize your reading experience.
- **Indie Side‑Project**: Scene suggestions, translation fixes and feature feedback are highly welcomed.

1. **Spoken‑language Learners**: Practise real‑life oral English for travel, study‑abroad and daily communication.
